    About

    I'm in my third year of software engineering in Montréal, somewhere between the compilers course that ruined my sleep schedule and the databases course that fixed it.

    Most of what I build starts as a small annoyance — a schedule that takes an hour to make by hand, a bus I keep missing — and turns into an excuse to learn something properly. I care about the parts nobody sees: the query that got 40× faster, the error message that finally says what went wrong.

    Outside of that I TA the first-year data structures course, which has taught me more about explaining things than any class I've taken.
